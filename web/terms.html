Beach, water and safety

This is the most important section here.

In 30A shows two kinds of beach flag information, and they are not the same thing. Some comes from public sources such as the South Walton Fire District and the National Weather Service. Some is reported by other app users, who are members of the public and not lifeguards. Community reports are labelled as such.

Neither is an official safety authority, neither is guaranteed to be current or correct, and conditions at the shoreline can change within minutes.

Do not use this app to decide whether it is safe to enter the water. Look at the flags physically flying at the beach access, read the posted signage, and follow lifeguards and South Walton Fire District personnel. Their word overrides anything shown here, always.

The same applies to parking counts, shuttle status, restroom conditions, wait times and opening hours. They are provided for convenience and may be out of date, incomplete or wrong.

Parking

Live parking space counts come from Park Walton County's public parking system and are shown with the time they were last updated. They are not a reservation, not a guarantee a space will be free when you arrive, and not a statement about whether you may park in a given lot. Payment, permits, time limits and enforcement are between you and the operator of that lot.
