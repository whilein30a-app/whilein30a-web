Delete your account

You can delete your In 30A account and its personal content in two ways.

In the app

Open In 30A, then Profile, then Account Details, then Delete Account. This is immediate and needs no email.

Without the app

If you no longer have In 30A installed, email in30a.info@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject "Account deletion request".

We will confirm the request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the account's email address, so that nobody can delete someone else's account.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Saved favourites
  • Itineraries and their stops
  • Reviews and ratings you wrote
  • Photo wall posts, comments and likes
  • Questions and answers you posted
  • Lost and found posts
  • Saved search alerts, notification settings and push token
  • Business suggestions and listing corrections
  • Bug reports and support messages

What is kept, and why

We would rather tell you this than promise an erasure we do not actually perform.

  • Community condition reports. Parking, shuttle, beach flag, restroom and wait time reports are submitted without being tied to an identity, on purpose, so that anyone can report a condition without signing up. There is no link from them back to you, which also means there is no way to find and remove yours. They remain as anonymous community data that other people rely on.
  • Booking records. Your name and phone number are cleared from any booking request, but the booking itself remains for the business's records. That business keeps its own copy under its own policy.
  • Replies other people wrote to something you posted. Those belong to them.
  • Routine backups and technical logs, for a short period until they age out in the normal cycle. They are not used for anything else meanwhile.
  • Aggregated figures that cannot be linked back to you.

Before you decide

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you would like to know exactly what is held about you first, email us and we will tell you.
